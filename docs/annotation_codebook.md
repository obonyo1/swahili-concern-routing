# Annotation Codebook
# Swahili Concern Classification System
# Author: Obonyo Paul Rapando, 169688

## Purpose

This codebook defines the rules for assigning one of four category
labels to each Swahili sentence in the training dataset.
Every sentence receives exactly one label.

## Categories

### Food Security
**Definition:** Sentences expressing lack of food, hunger, difficulty
accessing food, or food insecurity at household level.

**Include:**
- Direct expressions of hunger or lack of food
- Inability to afford food
- Food distribution access problems

**Exclude:**
- General agricultural news without food access concern
- Food prices without expressed difficulty affording food

**Examples:**
- "Hatuna chakula nyumbani" (We have no food at home)
- "Watoto wetu wanalala njaa" (Our children sleep hungry)

---

### Health
**Definition:** Sentences expressing illness, injury, lack of medical
care, or inability to access health services.

**Include:**
- Mentions of specific illnesses or symptoms
- Inability to access medicine or clinics
- Health emergencies

**Exclude:**
- General health education without personal health concern
- Nutrition content without illness

**Examples:**
- "Mama ana malaria lakini dawa hakuna" (Mother has malaria but there is no medicine)
- "Hospitali iko mbali sana" (The hospital is very far)

---

### Shelter
**Definition:** Sentences expressing housing instability, homelessness,
structural damage to homes, or inability to afford housing.

**Include:**
- Reports of houses collapsing or flooding
- Homelessness or eviction
- Inability to afford rent

**Exclude:**
- General construction news
- Infrastructure development without housing concern

**Examples:**
- "Nyumba yangu imebomoka" (My house has collapsed)
- "Tumefukuzwa nyumbani" (We have been evicted)

---

### Safety
**Definition:** Sentences expressing personal threat, crime, violence,
or insecurity in the community.

**Include:**
- Reports of theft, assault, or threats
- Fear of violence
- Community insecurity

**Exclude:**
- General news about crime elsewhere
- Historical accounts without personal safety concern

**Examples:**
- "Usiku kuna wezi mtaani mwetu" (At night there are thieves in our area)
- "Watoto hawezi kwenda shule kwa sababu ya hatari" (Children cannot go to school because of danger)
